# Genre Playbooks

## 1. Short-form (Reels / TikTok / Shorts), 15–60 s
- **Structure:** Hook (0–3 s) → Value in rapid beats → Payoff → Loop/CTA.
- **Cuts:** change visual every 1.5–3 s; punch-ins; captions word-by-word with emphasis; native sound/trend only if relevant.
- **Text:** hook line on-screen at 0.0; progress cue (1/3, 2/3) for lists; CTA last 2 s.
- **Audio:** voice forward; subtle music −18 dB; hits/risers on beats; loudness −14 LUFS.
- **Loop:** end frame/line leads naturally back to the first frame.
- **QC:** muted-watch test, thumb-zone safe area, 1080×1920, 30/60 fps.

## 2. YouTube Long-form
- Cold open (teaser/promise) → short branded sting (≤ 3 s, or none) → structured chapters → re-hooks → strong ending (not "that's it").
- Pattern interrupts every 20–40 s (B-roll, graphics, angle change, sound).
- Retention-aware: remove tangents; show the payoff early; end screens last 5–20 s.
- Deliver: title/thumbnail variants, chapters, captions, pinned comment.

## 3. Ads / Commercials (6 / 15 / 30 / 60 s)
- 0–2 s hook with product/problem; benefit by 5 s; proof/social proof; CTA + brand mark in the last 3–5 s.
- Sound-off first; captions; legible product shots; compliance (claims, disclosures, music licenses).
- Create cutdowns (30 → 15 → 6) and multiple aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, 16:9) from one master; test hooks.

## 4. Interviews / Corporate / Testimonials
- Build narrative from soundbites: hook quote → context → story → resolution → CTA.
- Multi-cam: cut on thought boundaries, reaction cutaways, B-roll that illustrates; ≤ 2 s cutaway on emotional peaks.
- Clean audio priority; consistent lower thirds; branded but understated; subtitles always.

## 5. Documentary
- Strong verité spine; VO only to bridge; ethics (context, consent, no manipulative editing).
- Let moments breathe; use sound design/ambience as emotional glue; archival treated consistently (aspect, grade, grain).
- Structure via index cards/story map; multiple fine-cuts before lock.

## 6. Trailer / Promo
- Cold open → premise → escalation → montage with accelerating cuts → button/stinger → title/date.
- Music dictates structure; mark hits (impacts) and cut to them; sound design silence beats before hits.
- Avoid spoilers; withhold the biggest beat; end with a memorable line/visual.

## 7. Event / Wedding / Highlights
- Capture emotional peaks (vows, laughs, reactions); sync with audio (speeches, music); montage to a licensed track.
- Deliver: highlight (2–4 min), teaser (30–60 s), full ceremony/speeches; color-match mixed-light venues; fix low-light noise.

## 8. Podcast / Talking-Head Video
- Multicam auto-switch by speaker with manual polish; punch-ins for variety; remove false starts/fillers; waveform/graphics only if useful.
- Pull 5–10 clips per episode (see short-form extraction); consistent captions & brand.
- Audio first: −16 LUFS, de-ess, noise cleanup; leveling per speaker.

## 9. Tutorial / Screen-record / Course
- Chunk by task; show result first; zoom and highlight UI; cut waits; callouts and keyboard overlays; chapters.
- Voice clarity > everything; consistent pacing; downloadable resources; captions.

## 10. Music Video / Cinematic
- Edit to rhythm and lyrics; mix performance with narrative; speed ramps and overlays used with intent; grade defines world; consider 2.39:1 letterbox only if composition supports it.

## 11. Gaming / Streams / Vlogs
- Highlight cutting: reaction cam + action; kill dead time; funny/emotional moments; memes sparingly with licensed assets; fast-paced captions; strong titles.
